Paste a link. Get the best quality.

A video is saved straight into Downloads. A playlist is saved into a folder named after that playlist. A channel is saved into a folder named after the channel: every video in upload order, and each playlist in its own folder. Each file uses the title from YouTube.

If YouTube blocks a long download, sign in to YouTube in Firefox and press Download again. Videos already saved are skipped.

ffmpeg isn’t available, so some videos may not be saved at the highest quality.
